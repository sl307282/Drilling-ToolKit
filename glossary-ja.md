# Japanese (日本語) Oil & Gas Drilling Terminology Glossary (`glossary-ja.md`)

This glossary establishes the standard Japanese terminology used across the **Drilling Toolkit** application. Every term is selected based on standard professional drilling engineering practice and industry nomenclature rather than literal machine translation.

---

| **Open Hole** | **裸坑 (オープンホール)** | Uncased rock borehole section below casing shoe |

| **Collapse Pressure / Rating** | **圧壊圧力 (コラプス耐圧)** | API external pressure crushing resistance |

## 3. Well Cementing
| English Term | Chosen Japanese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cementing** | **セメンティング (セメント作業)** | Well casing cementing operation |
| **Cement Job Planner** | **セメンティング作業プランナー** | Subsea / surface casing cement design module |
| **Cement Slurry** | **セメントスラリー** | Hydraulic cement mixed with water and chemical additives |
| **Lead Slurry** | **リードスラリー (低比重スラリー)** | Low density filler cement slurry placed high in annulus |
| **Tail Slurry** | **テイルスラリー (高強度スラリー)** | High density high compressive strength cement slurry across shoe |
| **Spacer** | **スペーサー (分離液)** | Weighted rheologically tuned fluid separating mud and cement |
| **Displacement Fluid** | **置換流体 (ディスプレースメント流体)** | Drilling mud or brine pumped behind plugs to displace slurry into annulus |
| **Annular Rise** | **環状部上昇高** | Vertical height of fluid column in annulus |
| **Top of Cement (TOC)** | **セメント上面 (TOC)** | Measured depth or TVD of uppermost annular cement column |

## 4. Fluids, Pressures & Hydraulics
| English Term | Chosen Japanese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mud Weight (MW)** | **泥水比重 (MW)** | Drilling fluid specific gravity / density (ppg, SG, kg/m³) |
| **Equivalent Circulating Density (ECD)** | **等価循環密度 (ECD)** | Static mud density plus annular hydraulic pressure loss |
| **Hydrostatic Pressure** | **静水圧** | Static pressure exerted by column of liquid (psi = 0.052 * MW * TVD) |
| **Shut-In Drill Pipe Pressure (SIDPP)** | **閉口時ドリルパイプ圧 (SIDPP)** | Gauge pressure on drillpipe after shutting in well on kick |
| **Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP)** | **閉口時ケーシング圧 (SICP)** | Gauge pressure on casing annulus after shutting in well on kick |
| **Kill Mud Weight (KMW)** | **キル泥水比重 (KMW)** | Calculated fluid density to balance formation pore pressure |
| **Leak Off Test (LOT)** | **リークオフテスト (地層耐圧試験 LOT)** | Formation fracture pressure test immediately below shoe |

## 6. Well Control & Stuck Pipe
| English Term | Chosen Japanese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Well Control** | **ウェルコントロール (噴出防止制御)** | Prevention and mitigation of formation influxes |
| **Kill Sheet** | **キルシート (圧井計算表)** | Worksheet computing pressures and volumes to kill well kick |
| **Kick** | **キック (地層流体流入)** | Unplanned influx of formation gas/oil/water into wellbore |
| **Blowout** | **ブローアウト (暴噴)** | Uncontrolled surface release of wellbore fluids |
| **Differential Sticking** | **差圧膠着 (ディファレンシャルスティッキング)** | Pipe stuck against permeable mudcake due to overbalanced hydrostatic pressure |
| **Mechanical Sticking** | **機械的膠着** | Pipe stuck in keyseat or packed cuttings bridge |
