# Simplified Chinese (简体中文) Oil & Gas Drilling Terminology Glossary (`glossary-zh.md`)

This glossary establishes the standard Simplified Chinese terminology used across the **Drilling Toolkit** application. Every term is selected based on standard professional drilling engineering practice and industry nomenclature rather than literal machine translation.

---

## 3. Well Cementing
| English Term | Chosen Simplified Chinese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cementing** | **固井作业** | Well casing cementing operation |
| **Cement Job Planner** | **固井方案设计器** | Subsea / surface casing cement design module |
| **Cement Slurry** | **水泥浆** | Hydraulic cement mixed with water and chemical additives |
| **Lead Slurry** | **领浆 / 低密度水泥浆 (Lead Slurry)** | Low density filler cement slurry placed high in annulus |
| **Tail Slurry** | **尾浆 / 高强度水泥浆 (Tail Slurry)** | High density high compressive strength cement slurry across shoe |
| **Spacer** | **隔离液 (Spacer)** | Weighted rheologically tuned fluid separating mud and cement |
| **Displacement Fluid** | **顶替液 (Displacement Fluid)** | Drilling mud or brine pumped behind plugs to displace slurry into annulus |
| **Annular Rise** | **环空上返高度** | Vertical height of fluid column in annulus |
| **Top of Cement (TOC)** | **水泥顶面高度 (TOC)** | Measured depth or TVD of uppermost annular cement column |

## 4. Fluids, Pressures & Hydraulics
| English Term | Chosen Simplified Chinese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mud Weight (MW)** | **泥浆密度 (MW)** | Drilling fluid specific gravity / density (ppg, SG, kg/m³) |
| **Equivalent Circulating Density (ECD)** | **当量循环密度 (ECD)** | Static mud density plus annular hydraulic pressure loss |
| **Hydrostatic Pressure** | **静液压力** | Static pressure exerted by column of liquid (psi = 0.052 * MW * TVD) |
| **Shut-In Drill Pipe Pressure (SIDPP)** | **关井钻杆压力 (SIDPP)** | Gauge pressure on drillpipe after shutting in well on kick |
| **Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP)** | **关井套管压力 (SICP)** | Gauge pressure on casing annulus after shutting in well on kick |
| **Kill Mud Weight (KMW)** | **压井泥浆密度 (KMW)** | Calculated fluid density to balance formation pore pressure |
| **Leak Off Test (LOT)** | **地层漏失试验 (LOT)** | Formation fracture pressure test immediately below shoe |

## 6. Well Control & Stuck Pipe
| English Term | Chosen Simplified Chinese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
