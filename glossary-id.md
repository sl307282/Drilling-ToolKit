# Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) Oil & Gas Drilling Terminology Glossary (`glossary-id.md`)

This glossary establishes the standard Indonesian terminology used across the **Drilling Toolkit** application. Every term is selected based on standard professional drilling engineering practice and industry nomenclature rather than literal machine translation.

---

| **Open Hole** | **Lubang Terbuka (Open Hole)** | Uncased rock borehole section below casing shoe |

## 3. Well Cementing
| English Term | Chosen Indonesian Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cementing** | **Sementasi Sumur** | Well casing cementing operation |
| **Cement Job Planner** | **Perencana Pekerjaan Sementasi** | Subsea / surface casing cement design module |
| **Cement Slurry** | **Slurry Semen** | Hydraulic cement mixed with water and chemical additives |
| **Lead Slurry** | **Lead Slurry (Slurry Pengisi)** | Low density filler cement slurry placed high in annulus |
| **Tail Slurry** | **Tail Slurry (Slurry Penutup)** | High density high compressive strength cement slurry across shoe |
| **Spacer** | **Cairan Spacer** | Weighted rheologically tuned fluid separating mud and cement |
| **Displacement Fluid** | **Fluida Pendorong (Displacement)** | Drilling mud or brine pumped behind plugs to displace slurry into annulus |
| **Annular Rise** | **Kenaikan Annular** | Vertical height of fluid column in annulus |
| **Top of Cement (TOC)** | **Puncak Semen (TOC)** | Measured depth or TVD of uppermost annular cement column |

## 4. Fluids, Pressures & Hydraulics
| English Term | Chosen Indonesian Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mud Weight (MW)** | **Berat Lumpur (MW)** | Drilling fluid specific gravity / density (ppg, SG, kg/m³) |
| **Equivalent Circulating Density (ECD)** | **Kerapatan Sirkulasi Setara (ECD)** | Static mud density plus annular hydraulic pressure loss |
| **Hydrostatic Pressure** | **Tekanan Hidrostatis** | Static pressure exerted by column of liquid (psi = 0.052 * MW * TVD) |
| **Shut-In Drill Pipe Pressure (SIDPP)** | **Tekanan Pipa Bor Tertutup (SIDPP)** | Gauge pressure on drillpipe after shutting in well on kick |
| **Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP)** | **Tekanan Casing Tertutup (SICP)** | Gauge pressure on casing annulus after shutting in well on kick |
| **Kill Mud Weight (KMW)** | **Berat Lumpur Matian (KMW)** | Calculated fluid density to balance formation pore pressure |
| **Leak Off Test (LOT)** | **Uji Kebocoran Formasi (LOT)** | Formation fracture pressure test immediately below shoe |

## 6. Well Control & Stuck Pipe
| English Term | Chosen Indonesian Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Well Control** | **Kontrol Sumur (Well Control)** | Prevention and mitigation of formation influxes |
| **Kill Sheet** | **Lembar Kontrol Sumur (Kill Sheet)** | Worksheet computing pressures and volumes to kill well kick |
| **Kick** | **Kick (Masuknya Fluida Formasi)** | Unplanned influx of formation gas/oil/water into wellbore |
| **Blowout** | **Semburan Liar (Blowout)** | Uncontrolled surface release of wellbore fluids |
| **Differential Sticking** | **Pipa Terjepit Tekanan Diferensial** | Pipe stuck against permeable mudcake due to overbalanced hydrostatic pressure |
| **Mechanical Sticking** | **Pipa Terjepit Mekanis** | Pipe stuck in keyseat or packed cuttings bridge |
