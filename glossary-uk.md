# Ukrainian Oil & Gas Drilling Terminology Glossary (`glossary-uk.md`)

This glossary establishes the standard Ukrainian terminology used across the **Drilling Toolkit** application. Every term is selected based on standard professional Ukrainian drilling engineering practice (ДСТУ and industry standard oilfield nomenclature) rather than literal machine translation.

---
