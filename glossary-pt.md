# Portuguese (Português) Oil & Gas Drilling Terminology Glossary (`glossary-pt.md`)

This glossary establishes the standard Portuguese terminology used across the **Drilling Toolkit** application. Every term is selected based on standard professional drilling engineering practice and industry nomenclature rather than literal machine translation.

---

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Subsea Wellhead** | **Cabeça de poço submarina** | Offshore seabed wellhead system |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Production Casing** | **Revestimento de produção** | Casing isolating target hydrocarbon reservoir |
| **Liner** | **Liner (Coluna perdida)** | Casing string suspended from previous shoe without extending to surface |
| **Casing Shoe** | **Sapata de revestimento** | Reinforced lower guide terminal of casing string |

| **Float Collar** | **Colar flutuador (Float collar)** | Check-valve casing coupling retaining wiper plugs |

| **Drill Collar (DC)** | **Comandos de perfuração (DC)** | Heavy thick-walled collars providing weight on bit (WOB) |
| **Open Hole** | **Poço aberto (Open hole)** | Uncased rock borehole section below casing shoe |
| **Burst Pressure / Rating** | **Resistência à pressão interna (Burst)** | API internal pressure yield capability |
| **Collapse Pressure / Rating** | **Resistência ao colapso externo** | API external pressure crushing resistance |

## 3. Well Cementing
| English Term | Chosen Portuguese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cementing** | **Cimentação de poços** | Well casing cementing operation |
| **Cement Job Planner** | **Planejador de cimentação** | Subsea / surface casing cement design module |
| **Cement Slurry** | **Pasta de cimento** | Hydraulic cement mixed with water and chemical additives |
| **Lead Slurry** | **Pasta de preenchimento (Lead)** | Low density filler cement slurry placed high in annulus |
| **Tail Slurry** | **Pasta de cauda (Tail)** | High density high compressive strength cement slurry across shoe |
| **Spacer** | **Fluido espaçador (Spacer)** | Weighted rheologically tuned fluid separating mud and cement |
| **Displacement Fluid** | **Fluido de deslocamento** | Drilling mud or brine pumped behind plugs to displace slurry into annulus |
| **Annular Rise** | **Altura de subida no anular** | Vertical height of fluid column in annulus |
| **Top of Cement (TOC)** | **Topo do cimento (TOC)** | Measured depth or TVD of uppermost annular cement column |

## 4. Fluids, Pressures & Hydraulics
| English Term | Chosen Portuguese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mud Weight (MW)** | **Massa específica da lama (MW)** | Drilling fluid specific gravity / density (ppg, SG, kg/m³) |
| **Equivalent Circulating Density (ECD)** | **Densidade equivalente de circulação (ECD)** | Static mud density plus annular hydraulic pressure loss |
| **Hydrostatic Pressure** | **Pressão hidrostática** | Static pressure exerted by column of liquid (psi = 0.052 * MW * TVD) |
| **Shut-In Drill Pipe Pressure (SIDPP)** | **Pressão de fechamento no tubo de perfuração (SIDPP)** | Gauge pressure on drillpipe after shutting in well on kick |
| **Shut-In Casing Pressure (SICP)** | **Pressão de fechamento no revestimento (SICP)** | Gauge pressure on casing annulus after shutting in well on kick |
| **Kill Mud Weight (KMW)** | **Densidade de lama de amortecimento (KMW)** | Calculated fluid density to balance formation pore pressure |
| **Leak Off Test (LOT)** | **Teste de absorção (LOT)** | Formation fracture pressure test immediately below shoe |

## 6. Well Control & Stuck Pipe
| English Term | Chosen Portuguese Term | Context / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Well Control** | **Controle de poço** | Prevention and mitigation of formation influxes |
| **Kill Sheet** | **Folha de controle de poço (Kill sheet)** | Worksheet computing pressures and volumes to kill well kick |
| **Kick** | **Kick (Influxo de fluido)** | Unplanned influx of formation gas/oil/water into wellbore |
| **Blowout** | **Erupção de poço (Blowout)** | Uncontrolled surface release of wellbore fluids |
| **Differential Sticking** | **Prisão diferencial da coluna** | Pipe stuck against permeable mudcake due to overbalanced hydrostatic pressure |
| **Mechanical Sticking** | **Prisão mecânica** | Pipe stuck in keyseat or packed cuttings bridge |
